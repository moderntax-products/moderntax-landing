How it works

Agents do the legwork. Practitioners stay accountable.

Agents that prepare authorizations, request and retrieve IRS records and keep every order moving, with credentialed practitioners (CAF-registered) on every IRS interaction and accountable for every request.

Start a verification → Talk to us
01
Our agents

Who does what on every order.

01 · Authorization

8821 agent

Prepares the Form 8821 and audits it before it goes out: the right designee, signer, title, tax years and forms. Once signed, it submits the authorization to the IRS so it can be recorded on the CAF (Centralized Authorization File, the IRS record of who a taxpayer has authorized).

02 · TDS

Transcript retrieval agent

Once the authorization is on file, requests the transcripts through TDS (the IRS Transcript Delivery System that tax professionals use) and attaches them to the order.

03 · SOR

Mailbox agent

Picks up transcripts the IRS delivers to the practitioner’s SOR (Secure Object Repository, the IRS secure mailbox for tax professionals) and matches each one to the right order and taxpayer.

04 · PPS

IRS call agent and call assistant

Places and holds Practitioner Priority Service (PPS) calls, the IRS phone line for tax professionals, then guides the credentialed practitioner who handles the live call.

05 · Follow-up

Monitoring and follow-up agents

Re-check years that came back unfiled and chase missing years, on the terms set in your quote, so an open item does not sit unnoticed.

06 · Quality of Earnings

QoE agent

Prepares an AI-assisted Quality of Earnings analysis from IRS-of-record data and borrower financials. A person reviews it before it reaches you.

02
People

A practitioner on every IRS interaction.

Agents prepare, request, retrieve and follow up. Credentialed, CAF-registered practitioners hold the authorizations, handle live IRS calls and are accountable for every request. Timing depends on the IRS and on when the signed authorization is on file.

03
Glossary

The IRS terms, in plain English.

CAF
Centralized Authorization File: the IRS record of the authorizations a taxpayer has given, and of the registered practitioners who hold them.
TDS
Transcript Delivery System: the IRS service tax professionals use to request transcripts.
SOR
Secure Object Repository: the IRS secure mailbox where some transcripts are delivered to a practitioner.
PPS
Practitioner Priority Service: the IRS phone line for tax professionals.
