01 · Before you order

What you need,
and what you get back.

Each order is one taxpayer, the form type and the tax years you need. Nothing goes to the IRS until the signed authorization is on the order.

Request requirements

To request transcripts

  • A Form 8821 signed by the taxpayer, naming ModernTax as designee. We pre-fill it; you collect the signature.
  • Taxpayer name and TIN: an SSN for a 1040, an EIN for a 1065, 1120, 1120-S or 941.
  • Form type and tax years, plus the fiscal year end for 1065, 1120 and 1120-S returns.
What a completed finding means

Reading the result

Each result shows what the IRS transcript held when we retrieved it: whether a return is on record for each year, balances and account activity, and the figures the IRS has on file. A definitive “no record of return filed” is a finding too: the IRS had no return on file for that year at retrieval.

Billing trigger

When an order is billed

Rejections are not billed. A bad EIN, a name mismatch or a signer not on record comes back with the field to fix. A definitive “no record of return filed” bills at the per-entity rate, because it is a real underwriting finding. Abandoned or incomplete pulls are not billed. Your quote or signed agreement defines covered services and billing terms.

How it works · Our agents

Agents do the legwork.
Practitioners stay accountable.

Agents that prepare authorizations, request and retrieve IRS records and keep every order moving, with credentialed practitioners (CAF-registered) on every IRS interaction and accountable for every request. How it works

Authorization

8821 agent

Prepares the Form 8821 and audits it before it goes out: the right designee, signer, title, tax years and forms. Once signed, it submits the authorization to the IRS so it can be recorded on the CAF (Centralized Authorization File, the IRS record of who a taxpayer has authorized).

TDS

Transcript retrieval agent

Once the authorization is on file, requests the transcripts through TDS (the IRS Transcript Delivery System that tax professionals use) and attaches them to the order.

SOR

Mailbox agent

Picks up transcripts the IRS delivers to the practitioner’s SOR (Secure Object Repository, the IRS secure mailbox for tax professionals) and matches each one to the right order and taxpayer.

PPS

IRS call agent and call assistant

Places and holds Practitioner Priority Service (PPS) calls, the IRS phone line for tax professionals, then guides the credentialed practitioner who handles the live call.

Follow-up

Monitoring and follow-up agents

Re-check years that came back unfiled and chase missing years, on the terms set in your quote, so an open item does not sit unnoticed.

Quality of Earnings

QoE agent

Prepares an AI-assisted Quality of Earnings analysis from IRS-of-record data and borrower financials. A person reviews it before it reaches you.
