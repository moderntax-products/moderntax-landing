Why ModernTax exists
MP

I started ModernTax by making thousands of calls to the IRS myself. It was daunting, but it is the fastest way to access real-time IRS information. This work was done so lenders didn’t have to do it themselves – some lenders staff this in-house, and it requires multiple staff members having IRS credentials and spending hours on the phone with the IRS. I’ll admit sitting on hold with the IRS is brutal, but it’s the work of hundreds of thousands of tax professionals annually.

This is a changing world, and voice AI is playing a big role in how this happens. While I don’t love a 60-minute wait time with the IRS, I’ve built my career on figuring out how technology will impact historically bad experiences. As AI takes on tasks, we asked the question of how it can help with getting real-time information for businesses to get access to capital.

At an SBA event this year, my world collided with how we help business owners at ModernTax. A business owner was hosting a happy hour at his brewery, and he described his process in securing a commercial loan to be able to purchase the property and make upgrades. One of the challenges was that his bank, unfortunately, at the time, was not using ModernTax, and he had to make 10 calls to the IRS to get access to old tax returns to close his loan. It made me happy to know that our human + AI-powered platform could have taken care of that job for him in 1 hour or less, and it is only getting faster. Our mission is to help business owners get and stay capital-ready.

Matt Parker
Founder, ModernTax
What we do today

We help commercial lenders, platforms, and marketplaces of all sizes handle the compliance work of accessing tax, accounting, and financial data in one central place, purely for the benefit of helping small businesses get access to capital. We’ve designed our solutions for all workflows, so lenders who use APIs and agents can use us, as well as processing, underwriting, and closing teams via a portal and dashboard.
