01 · The platform

Verify. Monitor.
Recover.

The ModernTax Compliance Platform puts one IRS authorization behind every product: verify a taxpayer before you fund, watch the file after closing, recover what the IRS is holding — and build any of it into your own product. A portal for the teams doing the work, an API for the platforms originating it.

Verify

Know the IRS standing before you fund.

Filed and unfiled years, balances due and IRS-of-record figures for any business or individual, authorized with a digital 8821 — no fax — and back the same day.

  • Compliance Verification — filed years, balances, IRS-of-record data
  • Employment & Income Verification — W-2 and 1099 wage & income
  • Payroll Liability (941) Report — quarter-by-quarter payroll-tax exposure
  • QoE Screening — which SBA loans need a Quality of Earnings review

Monitor

Catch the year that finally gets filed.

Scheduled IRS re-pulls on the entities you choose. When a year that was unfiled at closing posts as filed, you hear about it — and you pay only when a pull returns something new.

  • Continuous Monitoring — $39.99 per landed update, opt-in per entity
