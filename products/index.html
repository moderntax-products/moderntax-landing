Where we started

When we began our journey, we heard from lots of lenders: we need a way to scale our IRS operation, but we have hit snags. Others came to us because their vendor didn’t answer support questions for weeks; another had all of their customers get notifications in the mail from the IRS on behalf of the vendor. A lender’s main goal is to compliantly fund loans and serve customers.

02
Product catalog

Beyond transcripts

Our offering starts with tax transcripts, but it does not end there. In 2026, standard SBA Express offerings under $350,000 begin to require tax records. Heading into 2027, many of our customers have additional compliance requirements, and their borrowers need to purchase QoE reports for mergers and acquisitions loans over $3 million. Non-government-guaranteed lending for business is growing, and as rates rise, we are seeing more compliance requirements. We’ve built credibility by providing accurate and affordable tax transcripts, and now we help with our new QoE agent built for business owners and our full-service QoE offering in partnership with licensed CPA firms.

03
Product catalog

Capital readiness

Our mission starts and ends with business owners, so our offering can’t stop at tax transcript delivery like most of our competitors. With our technology and human expertise, we help business owners and their accountants and CPAs discover what can be catastrophic issues that stop funding. We like to think of our business as more of a capital readiness business for small business owners than compliance technology for lenders.

04
Product catalog

What you can order today

Lenders and their teams can order transcripts across all IRS forms. These records are synthesized to help their teams read the insights in real time and understand the impact on the use case. We also help our customers monitor particular entities, complete discovery, and get updates that provide more context. We are launching more agents to help our customers do their jobs better.
