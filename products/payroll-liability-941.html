Payroll Liability (941) Report

The liability an income transcript won’t show.

A business can be current on its income tax return and still be behind on payroll withholding. The 941 report reads the IRS account transcripts quarter by quarter and surfaces outstanding employment-tax balances before they become your problem.

What you get

Payroll-tax exposure, quarter by quarter.

01

Every quarter

Form 941 account transcripts for each quarter in the years you request — filed or not, paid or not.

02

Outstanding balances

Unpaid payroll-tax balances, penalties and interest as the IRS has them assessed.

03

Trust-fund exposure

Withheld taxes that were not paid over carry personal exposure for responsible persons — see it at underwriting, not in a demand letter.

04

Alongside income verification

Order it on the same taxpayer as a Compliance Verification so both views sit on one order.

How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Add 941 to the order

    Choose Form 941 and the years to cover on any business order.

  2. We pull each quarter

    Account transcripts for every quarter, using the same signed 8821.

  3. Read the report

    Quarter-by-quarter filing status and balances, as PDF and structured JSON.
