Continuous Monitoring

Catch the year that finally gets filed.

Scheduled IRS re-pulls on the entities you choose. When a year that was unfiled at closing posts as filed, you are told — and you are billed only when a pull returns something new.

01
What you get

Closing conditions that clear themselves.

01

Opt in per entity

Monitoring is never automatic. You enroll the entities you want watched, at intake or any time after.

02

Scheduled re-pulls

We re-check the IRS on a schedule for the years that came back with no record, using the 8821 already on file.

03

Unfiled → filed flag

When a missing year posts, the new transcript is attached to your original order and you are notified.

04

Billed on new records only

A check that finds nothing new costs nothing. The fee applies only when a pull returns a fresh transcript.

05

Keep or stop

Once the condition clears, you choose whether to keep watching or deactivate. Nothing re-pulls without your opt-in.

06

Same order, same record

Monitoring results land on the order your team already works from, in the portal and the API.

02
How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Enroll

    Tick monitoring on the entity at intake, or switch it on later from the order.

  2. We re-check

    Scheduled IRS re-pulls target the years that were unfiled on the original order.

  3. A year posts

    The fresh transcript is attached to your order and the year is flagged as now filed.

  4. You decide

    Keep monitoring or deactivate — the enrollment never renews behind your back.
