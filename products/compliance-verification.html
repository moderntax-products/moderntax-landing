Compliance Verification

Know a borrower's IRS standing before you fund.

Which years are filed, which are not, what is owed, and what the IRS actually has on record for revenue and income — for any business or individual, authorized with a digital 8821 and delivered by API or portal, same day.

01
What you get

One order answers the questions underwriting actually asks.

01

Filed & unfiled years

Year by year, whether a return is on record with the IRS — including the years that come back “no record”, with the reason, so a missing return is caught before closing rather than after.

02

Balances & account activity

Record of Account data: balance due, assessed penalties and interest, payments and the IRS transaction codes behind them.

03

IRS-of-record figures

Gross receipts, total income, net income and total tax as the IRS has them — ready to compare against the returns the borrower handed you.

04

Digital 8821, no fax

Enter the taxpayer once and get a pre-filled Form 8821 with only the signature left. Signed forms are uploaded, not faxed.

05

API-first delivery

REST intake, webhook or polling results, PDF transcripts plus structured JSON and a compliance summary: filed up, owes the IRS, as of when.

06

Same-day turnaround

Same-day IRS transcript turnaround once the signed 8821 is in, with every order visible in the portal and the API alike.

IRS forms covered

1040 1065 1120 1120-S 941 Return Transcript Record of Account Account Transcript Entity Transcript
02
How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Submit the taxpayer

    Portal form, CSV upload or one API call. Business returns state the fiscal year end; years and form type travel with the order.

  2. Sign the 8821

    A pre-filled 8821 comes back with only the signature open. It goes through your own closing process — signed, uploaded, no fax.

  3. We pull from the IRS

    The request routes to the IRS the same day and is tracked to the minute in the portal and the API.

  4. Read the result

    PDF transcripts, structured JSON and a compliance summary land on the order, by webhook or poll.
