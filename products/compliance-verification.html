Compliance Verification

Know a borrower's IRS standing before you fund.

Which years are filed, which are not, what is owed, and what the IRS has on record for revenue and income — for businesses and individuals, authorized with a signed Form 8821 and delivered in the portal or by API.

01
What you get

One order answers the questions underwriting actually asks.

01

Filed & unfiled years

Year by year, whether a return is on record with the IRS — including the years that come back “no record”, with the reason, so a missing return is caught before closing rather than after.

02

Balances & account activity

Record of Account data: balance due, assessed penalties and interest, payments and the IRS transaction codes behind them.

03

IRS-of-record figures

Gross receipts, total income, net income and total tax as the IRS has them — ready to compare against the returns the borrower handed you.

04

Pre-filled Form 8821

Enter the taxpayer once and get a pre-filled Form 8821 with only the signature left. Upload the signed form to the order.

05

Portal or API delivery

PDF transcripts plus structured JSON and a compliance summary, on the order in the portal and through the API.

06

What a result means

A result reflects the IRS transcript at retrieval. Keep it with your loan file and use it alongside the borrower’s own documents.

IRS forms covered

1040 1065 1120 1120-S 941 Return Transcript Record of Account Account Transcript Entity Transcript
02
How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Submit the taxpayer

    Portal form, CSV upload or one API call. Business returns state the fiscal year end; years and form type travel with the order.

  2. Sign the 8821

    A pre-filled 8821 comes back with only the signature open. It goes through your own closing process, then is uploaded to the order.

  3. We request from the IRS

    Once the signed 8821 is in, the request goes to the IRS and its status is tracked in the portal and the API.

  4. Read the result

    PDF transcripts, structured JSON and a compliance summary land on the order, by webhook or poll.
