Check Reissue Recovery

Get the refund check that never arrived.

When an IRS refund check is returned undelivered, the money sits at the IRS until someone fixes the address of record and asks for a reissue. We do that work and track the check to delivery.

Start free in the sandbox → Talk to us

Part of the ModernTax Compliance Platform · See all products

01
What you get

From returned check to reissued check.

01

Confirm the return

The account transcript shows when a refund check was issued and returned. We read it before we start, so you know what is recoverable.

02

Fix the address of record

We prepare the IRS change-of-address filing (Form 8822-B for businesses) so the reissue goes to the right place.

03

Request the reissue

We contact the IRS to request the check be reissued and follow it through.

04

Track to delivery

We re-check the account until the reissue posts and confirm with you when it is on its way.

02
How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Verify

    Pull the account transcript to confirm a returned check and the amount.

  2. Correct

    Update the address of record with the IRS.

  3. Request

    Ask the IRS to reissue the check.

  4. Confirm

    Track the reissue to delivery.
