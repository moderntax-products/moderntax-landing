Employment & Income Verification

Review IRS-reported wage and income information.

Talk to us → How it works

Part of the ModernTax Compliance Platform · See all products

Building on the API? Explore the sandbox · synthetic data; the API docs list what it supports.

Employment & Income Verification: We provide consent-based employment and income verification services to HR companies, K12 education platforms, and mortgage lenders. These companies want wage-and-income data.

Employment and income verification is a part of how several industries validate credit risks, affordability, and eligibility. At Moderntax we help not only lenders but wealth managers, landlords, educational providers, employers, and government benefit agencies use tax information to supplement other data sources like payroll, bank feeds and other alternative sources.

We provide our partners with permissioned access to wage and income records, payroll records on their clients on an annual or quarterly basis.

Our data does not track more granular information like benefits, overtime, direct deposits and commissions.

The process is consistent with our other workflows in that the partner collects the consent authorizations and securely passes it through to us. We then utilize it to access the requested information via an API or report.

01
What you get

What the wage and income transcript shows.

01

W-2 wages

Employer, wages and withholding as reported to the IRS on Form W-2, year by year.

02

1099 income

1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-K and other information returns, by payer, as reported to the IRS.

03

Multi-year history

Request several tax years in one order to compare what was reported over time.

04

Signed Form 8821

The individual signs a pre-filled 8821, which you upload to the order.

05

Structured JSON

Parsed employer and payer records alongside the PDF transcript.

06

Known limits

Information returns can post late or be missing, especially for the current processing year. A transcript does not show current employment.

IRS forms covered

W-2 1099-NEC 1099-MISC 1099-K Wage & Income Transcript
02
How it works

From order to answer.

  1. Submit the individual

    Name, SSN and tax years — through the portal or the API.

  2. Consent by 8821

    The individual signs the pre-filled 8821; you upload it.

  3. Receive the record

    Wage & Income transcript as PDF plus structured JSON, to use with your other verification sources.
