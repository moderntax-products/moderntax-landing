Upload signed 8821s to the IRS

3 signed 8821s are waiting to go to the CAF unit. Sign in with ID.me once; the agent fills in and uploads each one at irs.gov, then checks when it posts.

    How it works
    • The IRS window opens for the holder. They sign in with ID.me; that is the only step that needs them.
    • The agent answers the IRS form and attaches each signed 8821. Each confirmation number is saved on the case.
    • The agent never ticks an attestation for the holder. If the IRS asks them to confirm a statement, the agent stops so they can tick it themselves.
    • After 5, 7 and 10 business days the agent checks the IRS Transcript Delivery System to see whether the 8821 has posted. Once it has, the pull runs on its own.
    portal.moderntax.io/request · Case EX-1048

    Case EX-1048 · 1 client

    1. 1

      Authorization on file

      The case sits in the holder's queue with their CAF on it. New clients go through step 6 first.

    2. 2

      Transcript Delivery System

      When the 8821 is on the CAF file, the agent requests transcripts through TDS under the holder's own access, and they often come back in minutes.

    3. 3

      Practitioner Priority, when needed

      For anything TDS can't return, the agent calls the PPS line inside the holder's hours and books a callback. It waits on hold and connects the holder when an IRS agent picks up.

    4. 4

      Transcripts land on the case

      The pickup agent opens the holder's SOR mailbox with them, attaches each transcript to the right case by tax ID, and completes the order.

    Where to watch it
    • The holder sees every step on their dashboard. The firm owner sees all cases on the firm account, labeled by case number.
    • Cancelled orders aren't billed.
