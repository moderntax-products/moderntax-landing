Employment & HR Verification

Review IRS-reported wage and income information.

Wage and income transcripts show information reported to the IRS. Records may be incomplete, especially for the current processing year, and do not establish current employment. Use the results with other verification sources.

Requirements

What the Wage & Income Transcript Shows

Information that employers and payers reported to the IRS, by tax year. Use it alongside your other verification sources.

01

W-2 Wages

Employer name, wages and withholding as reported to the IRS on Form W-2. Current-year records may be incomplete while the IRS is still processing them.

02

1099 Income

1099-NEC, 1099-MISC and 1099-K information returns as reported to the IRS, by payer.

03

Multi-Year History

Request several tax years in one order to compare what was reported over time.

The platform

How ModernTax Helps

API-First Design

RESTful API with webhook notifications. Submit verification requests programmatically and receive structured JSON results.

Employer Detection

Automatically identify employers from W-2 data, including EIN matching and employer name normalization across tax years.

PEO Flagging

Flags when a Professional Employer Organization (PEO) appears as the W-2 employer of record on the transcript.

Structured Data

Parsed, structured JSON responses with employer details, income amounts, and filing metadata — no PDF scraping required.

Use With Other Sources

A transcript shows what was reported to the IRS. It does not establish current employment; use it with your other verification sources.

Webhook Notifications

Get notified the moment results are ready. No polling required — integrate directly into your existing workflow automation.
