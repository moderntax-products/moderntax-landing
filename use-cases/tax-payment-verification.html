01
The problem

The Payment Visibility Gap

Tax preparation platforms help members estimate and file taxes, but the payment step happens outside the platform. This creates blind spots that lead to missed payments, surprise IRS notices, and frustrated members.

01

Members Leave to Pay

Members prepare returns inside your platform, then navigate to IRS.gov or mail a check. You have no confirmation the payment was actually made.

02

No Payment Confirmation

IRS payments take days to weeks to process. Without transcript data, there is no reliable way to confirm a payment posted to the right account and tax year.

03

Surprise IRS Notices

Members receive CP14 balance due notices months later with no warning. Your support team fields calls about issues you had no visibility into.

02
The platform

How ModernTax Helps

Account Balance Review

Pull IRS account transcripts to review balances across the tax years you request, as shown at retrieval.

Posted Payment Review

Review payments posted to the account and tax year through IRS transaction codes. A payment that has not posted yet will not appear; the retrieval time is not when a payment was made or posted.

Historical Transcript Data

Request several tax years in one order to review outstanding balances, posted payments and filing history across years.

03
Workflow

How It Works

Step 1

Authorize via 8821

A Form 8821 signed by the taxpayer authorizes ModernTax to receive account transcripts for the tax years and forms it lists.

Step 2

We Pull Account Transcripts

ModernTax retrieves account transcripts directly from the IRS, including balances, payment history, and transaction codes for each tax year.

Step 3

Review

Receive structured data showing payment status and balances as shown in the transcript at retrieval. Confirm unresolved payments with the appropriate records.

04
Edge cases

Built for Complex Tax Scenarios

Business tax payments are more complex than personal returns.

Business Entity Payments

Business accounts have limited online access compared to personal accounts. An account transcript shows the payments the IRS has posted to the business account.

Joint Filing Attribution

Married filing jointly creates payment attribution questions. The account transcript shows payments at the account level, which helps review how they were applied to the return.

Multi-Year Balances

Members may owe across multiple tax years with payments applied inconsistently. Request each open year to review balances and posted payments side by side.
