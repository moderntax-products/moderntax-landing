Text Message Terms

Effective October 13, 2026

These terms cover ModernTax Work Alerts, the text-message (SMS) program run by Rapidly Financial, Inc. (“ModernTax”, “we”, “us”) for the tax professionals who work orders on the ModernTax portal.

What the program sends

Operational messages about work assigned to you in the ModernTax portal: IRS phone calls booked for you and callback times, orders ready for you to work, short links to sign in to your own IRS account, and replies to messages you send us. You can also text commands such as STATUS and GO to our number. We do not send marketing messages.

How you join

You opt in on your profile page in the ModernTax portal by entering your mobile number and ticking the text-message consent box. The box is unchecked by default. Changing your number requires a new opt-in. Your consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Frequency and cost

  • Message frequency varies with your assigned work.
  • Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Help and opting out

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. We will send one message confirming you are unsubscribed. Reply START to join again.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Support: support@moderntax.io

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle information.

Changes

We may update these terms. We will post the new version here with a new effective date.
