FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can we change scope later?
Yes — talk to your account team. Your master services agreement defines covered services, billing and cancellation terms, including how a change in scope works.
How does Continuous Monitoring work?
Ask us about follow-up transcript requests for a missing filing. The check schedule, charges and stopping rule are set in your MSA before enrollment.
Do I get charged for a rejection or a no-record?
Rejections are never billed — bad EIN, name mismatch, signer not on record, all free, and we tell you the specific field to fix. A definitive "no record of return filed" is billable under your MSA, because it’s a real underwriting finding: it tells you that year is unfiled. Abandoned or incomplete pulls are not billed.
Who generates the 8821 — and do you contact our borrower?
You enter the entity details once and download a fully pre-populated 8821 — taxpayer name, TIN, address, form, years, and our CAF information already filled in, with only the signature and date left blank. It goes into your loan docs and through your own signature process. We never email, call, or text your borrower unless you explicitly ask us to route signatures for you.
